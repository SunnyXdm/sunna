Remote desktop for your own computers

Your other computers, right here.

Click a computer and you’re at its screen, with its sound, your keyboard and your clipboard. Your devices connect to each other directly: no accounts, and nothing passes through a server of ours.

01 Your computers

Every computer, as you left it.

Each computer shows what was on its screen when you left, and whether you can reach it now. Gallery puts them side by side; Devices, Wall, Space and Type arrange the same computers four other ways.

The picture is taken once, as you leave a session, and stays on your device. You can blur it or turn it off.

Computers view

02 Setting up

Set it up once. Sharing is one switch.

  1. Share a computer

    On the computer you want to reach, open Settings and turn on Share this computer. It stays shared after you close the window.

    The switch works on Linux today. It is written for the Mac and waiting for its first test on one.

  2. Add it on your other devices

    Sharing gives you a link, and a QR code for your phone. Paste the link into the app, or scan the code.

    sunna://100.101.102.103?key=7f3a…&id=Qm9v…

    The link carries that computer’s identity and the key that lets you in. Treat it like a password.

  3. Open it

    When the computer shows Ready, click or tap it. Its desktop opens full screen, and Disconnect in the session menu brings you back.

03 Typing

Typing that keeps up.

Video takes time to encode, most of all on a computer without a GPU. So Sunna sends small changes, like the letter you just typed, as exact image tiles on a lane of their own, ahead of the video.

04 After a restart

Restart it, then sign in from here.

Set up for the whole machine, a Linux host starts before anyone signs in. After a reboot you see the login screen, type your password, and land on your desktop in the same session.

Experimental. Proven with LightDM and SDDM in test machines, not yet on real hardware.

05 Updates

Update a computer without going to it.

The app shows which of your computers are behind, and updates one when you ask.

Works for a computer set up with the install command. One installed from a package updates with the rest of its system.

Update stage
Signed
The computer checks the release’s signature before it installs anything.
One step
It switches version in a single step that can’t half-happen.
A way back
If the new version doesn’t start, it returns to the one before.
Never mid-session
It waits until nobody is connected before it restarts.

06 What you need

Three things to have.

  1. Your network

    Sunna works on any network you trust. With Tailscale, free for personal use, your computers are reachable from anywhere and closed to everyone else. Keep a shared computer off the public internet.

    Tailscale ↗

  2. The computer you’ll reach

    A Mac or a Linux computer. Install Sunna on it and turn on sharing.

  3. The device you’ll use

    A Mac, a Linux computer or an Android phone. Install Sunna on it, then add the computer by its link.

07 Get Sunna

Pick your device.

Sunna goes on both devices: the computer you’ll reach and the one in your hands. Pick each of them here and follow its steps.

Your device

Mac with Apple silicon

An M1 or later, on macOS 13 or newer.

Download Sunna.dmg ↓
Reach your computers from it
Yes
Reach it from your other devices
Yes, new

  1. Install it

    Open the .dmg and drag Sunna into Applications.

  2. Open it past the warning

    Sunna isn’t signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS stops it the first time. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

    On macOS 14 and earlier, right-click Sunna and choose Open instead.

  3. Reach a computer

    Choose Add a Computer and paste the link from the computer you’re sharing. Click it when it shows Ready.

  4. Or share this Mac

    In Settings, turn on Share this computer. Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when Sunna asks.

    Sharing from the app is new on the Mac and hasn’t had a full test yet. Share a Mac has the details.

This copy doesn’t update itself. When there’s a new release, download the .dmg again; macOS may then ask for its permissions again. Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac: a Chip line means Apple silicon.

Reach your computers from it
Yes
Reach it from your other devices
Yes

  1. Install the app

    Download it, then run this in the folder it landed in:

    sudo apt install ./sunna.deb

    On Fedora: sudo dnf install ./sunna.rpm. On any other distribution, or without root, use the install command at the top of this page.

  2. Reach a computer

    Open Sunna, choose Add a Computer and paste the link from the computer you’re sharing. Click it when it shows Ready.

  3. Or share this computer

    In Settings, turn on Share this computer. It gives you the link, and a QR code for your phone.

    A server with no screen takes sunna-host instead of the app: install it and run sunna-host setup. Share a Linux computer has the details.

A package updates with the rest of your system. A copy from the install command updates itself. On ARM, build Sunna from source.

Android

A phone or tablet on Android 10 or newer.

Download sunna.apk ↓
Reach your computers from it
Yes
Reach it from your other devices
No

  1. Install it

    Open sunna.apk on the phone. Android asks once whether your browser may install apps: allow it, then tap Install.

  2. Add a computer

    On the computer you’re sharing, open Sunna’s Settings and choose Show QR Code. Scan it with the phone’s camera.

  3. Open it

    Tap the computer when it shows Ready.

Sunna checks for a new version once a day, and Android asks you before installing it. Android guide
